SANVS is the practice of Thakor Yuvrajsinh — a designer working across identity, photography and film. The work moves between disciplines by necessity: a brand rarely lives in one medium, and the studio is built to follow it wherever it needs to go, from a mark on a business card to a frame in a finished film.
